# Demo Video Plan (≤ 30 seconds)

**Goal:** in 30 s, a judge should see (1) real NASA crater data, (2) the one big decision, (3) its consequence.
**Format:** 1920×1080 screen recording, no voice needed (judges often watch muted), big on-screen captions.
**Crater:** Faustini (shortest route, about 11 km) · **Payload:** Neutron + Drill + Backup.

## Shot list
| Time | Show (what's on screen) | Caption (add in editor) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Title screen, 3D orbit over Shackleton | **Lunar Mission Designer** · Team thegoatthox |
| 3–7 s | Crater picker, scroll the cards (LOLA tags visible) | Six real south-pole craters, built from NASA LRO laser-altimeter data |
| 7–11 s | Loadout: un-pack Backup → forecast shows **Stranded**; re-pack Backup → **Full success** | The choice: one more instrument, or a backup battery? |
| 11–16 s | Driving down the blue beacons into the dark; HUD shows PERMANENT SHADOW, NO SIGNAL, DRAINING | No sunlight. No signal. Heaters on. |
| 16–20 s | Battery fault alarm → "Route power through the backup" | Then something breaks. |
| 20–24 s | Cold trap: press E, Drill → "Frost on the drill bit. That's ice!" | Drill the cold trap |
| 24–28 s | Climbing out → "Earth is back in view. Uploading…" → debrief **FULL SUCCESS** + energy breakdown | Climbing out costs m·g·h, and the backup got us home |
| 28–30 s | End card (debrief or title) | Play free: xuwangzitai.github.io/lunar-mission-designer |

Speed up the driving clips 2–4× in the editor; the real drive takes about 1–2 minutes.

## Recording (Windows 11, nothing to install)
1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge, full screen (F11), browser zoom 100 %.
2. Press **Win + Alt + R** to start/stop recording the window (Xbox Game Bar). Clips save to `Videos\Captures`.
3. Record several short clips instead of one long take:
   - title + picker + loadout (~25 s)
   - drive down to the cold trap, including the battery fault and the drill (as long as it takes)
   - climb out + debrief
   The battery fault is random (when you first enter the shadow, or at the first science run). If it doesn't show up in the shadow, just fly again.
4. Edit in **Clipchamp** (built into Windows): trim, speed up the driving (2–4×), add the captions above as large text, check the total is **≤ 30.0 s**, export **1080p**.

## Upload
1. YouTube → Create → Upload video → visibility **Unlisted** (anyone with the link can watch, no sign-in).
   (Google Drive also works: share as "Anyone with the link".)
2. Open the link in a private/incognito window to confirm it plays without signing in.
3. Paste the full URL into **Project Demonstration** on the Space Apps project page → **Save Progress**.
